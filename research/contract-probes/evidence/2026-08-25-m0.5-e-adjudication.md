## 5. Raw body and canonicalization

The normative production rule remains unchanged: verification consumes the exact original raw body bytes. X1 reproduced against the exact captured bytes.

For this observation, JSON parsing succeeded and the 459-byte raw body equaled the 459-byte ordinary parse/stringify result byte-for-byte. Therefore the provider distinction between signing arbitrary original bytes and signing a provider serialization/canonical representation remains U. The observation does not prove provider canonicalization, does not make `JSON.parse`/`JSON.stringify` safe for verification, and does not weaken REQ-WH-01.
