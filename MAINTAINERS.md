# Maintainers

This document lists the active maintainers of the `nestjs-chapa` project and their responsibilities.

## Active Maintainers

- **Sye** ([@Sye-1321](https://github.com/Sye-1321)) - Initial and sole maintainer.

## Authority Model

Under the current solo-maintainer model, Sye (`Sye-1321`) initially holds:
- project/product decision authority;
- merge authority;
- security-response authority;
- npm release authority.

While there is only one maintainer, an external approving reviewer is not required for pull requests to ensure that normal solo-maintainer merges remain practical. Additional review requirements may be introduced when more maintainers exist.

## Succession and Policy

For policies regarding the addition or removal of maintainers, conflict resolution, and maintainer succession, please refer to [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
