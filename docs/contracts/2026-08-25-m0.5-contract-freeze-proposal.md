### Inventory interpretation rules

- A provider observation and its adjudication are complementary roles, not two independent provider observations.
- A plan or documentation review may be a D-source but does not prove execution or provider behavior.
- A local-only vector supports reproducibility of an already observed construction; it cannot independently promote that construction.
- Mixed-state adjudications remain explicitly `candidate-V / U`; no row compresses them to V.
- The B plan is the retained maintainer authority for the three narrow V fixture claims. No reviewer identity or review date beyond what the committed artifacts retain is asserted.

## Manifest Extension Decision — NOT ADOPTED

The maintainer approved `test/fixtures/chapa/manifest.json` as a contract fixture manifest only. Its plain JSON array and three M0.5-B `ContractFixtureManifestEntry` objects remain unchanged. Appendix F's required `fixturePath` and D/V/U semantics remain unchanged.

C/D/E Markdown observations, adjudications, safety decisions, and synthetic-vector tests are not fixtures and will not be added to that manifest. Their authoritative index is this proposal. Atomic claim rows link one provider observation to its classification authority without counting the adjudication as another observation. Synthetic vectors are local corroboration only. `candidate-V` remains an analysis state here until F5; it is not encoded into Appendix F as `evidence: "U"` plus another field. At F5 each candidate claim must be promoted to V or explicitly retained/deferred as U.
